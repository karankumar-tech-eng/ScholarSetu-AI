# 🎓 ScholarSetu AI

An AI-powered Scholarship & Government Benefits Platform designed to help students discover scholarships, government schemes, educational benefits, and admission opportunities through a single intelligent platform.

## 🚀 Project Overview

Many students miss scholarships and government benefits because of a lack of awareness, complex eligibility requirements, and scattered information sources.

ScholarSetu AI solves this problem by providing an intelligent platform that recommends scholarships, checks eligibility, tracks applications, verifies documents, and sends deadline alerts.

The platform aims to improve access to educational opportunities for school students, college students, and competitive exam aspirants across India.
